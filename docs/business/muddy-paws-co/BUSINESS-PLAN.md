# Muddy Paws Co. — Business Plan

**Prepared:** 12 August 2026
**Founder base:** Chessington, Surrey, KT9 (remote/online business — no premises required)
**Status:** Side hustle alongside full-time employment

## 1. The Idea

A UK print-on-demand (POD) apparel and gifts brand for dog owners: hoodies, t-shirts,
tote bags, mugs, and dog bandanas carrying witty, warm British dog-walking humour
("Muddy Paws, Happy Heart", "Sunday Best Behaved", "Wet Dog Smell Is My Perfume").

No stock is held. Orders are printed and shipped by a POD supplier only after a
customer buys, so there is no upfront inventory risk.

## 2. Why This Business

- **Low capital, low risk** — POD means no inventory spend; you only pay production
  cost per order, after the customer has already paid you.
- **Fits a side-hustle schedule** — design and marketing work happens in evenings/
  weekends; fulfilment is entirely outsourced to the POD supplier.
- **Evergreen, highly shareable niche** — UK dog ownership is large and growing, and
  dog content performs exceptionally well on short-form video (Reels/TikTok/YouTube
  Shorts), which is free, organic marketing.
- **Reuses existing skills in this workspace** — the YouTube content-automation
  pipeline already built in this repo (`apps/youtube`) can be repointed at a dog/pet
  content niche to drive organic traffic at near-zero marginal cost.
- **Fully online** — no premises, no food hygiene rating, no physical location
  dependency on Chessington.

## 3. Target Customer

UK dog owners, roughly 25–55, who buy apparel/gifts for themselves or as presents for
other dog owners. Values: humour, quality basics (not cheap-feeling prints), and a
brand that "gets" dog-owner culture (muddy walks, vet bills, dog hair on everything).

## 4. Business Structure & Registration

Start as a **Sole Trader** — the correct structure for testing a new business:

- Registration with HMRC is **free** (register for Self Assessment at gov.uk before
  5 October following the tax year you start trading, or once income exceeds the
  £1,000 trading allowance).
- No Companies House involvement or fee at this stage.
- Convert to a **Limited Company** later if profit and risk exposure justify it —
  online incorporation is **£100** from 1 February 2026 (up from £50), plus a **£50/yr**
  confirmation statement filing fee. Revisit this once trading consistently, typically
  once profits approach ~£30–40k/yr where the tax efficiency starts to outweigh the
  admin overhead.

## 5. Startup Costs (bootstrapped)

| Item | Cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Sole trader registration | £0 | HMRC Self Assessment |
| Domain name | ~£10/yr | e.g. muddypawsco.co.uk |
| Online store | £0–£25/mo | Etsy (pay-per-listing, no monthly fee) to start; Shopify (~£25/mo) once validated |
| POD supplier account | £0 | Printful/Printify — free to connect, pay per order only |
| Design tool | £0–£10/mo | Canva free tier is enough to start |
| Initial marketing | £0 | Organic short-form video; optional £50–100 paid ad test once designs are validated |
| **Total to launch** | **~£10–50** | Realistic bootstrapped start |

## 6. Revenue Model & Margins

POD apparel typically retails £22–28 for a hoodie against a £14–16 base production
cost, giving roughly **35–45% gross margin per unit** before platform/payment fees
(Etsy ~6.5% transaction fee + payment processing; Shopify ~2–3% payment fee).
Lower-cost items (mugs, totes) usually carry a similar or slightly better margin.

## 7. Marketing Plan

- **Organic short-form video** — dog-walk clips, "what my dog thinks of the rain"
  humour, product reveals. Reuse/repoint the YouTube automation pipeline already in
  this repo for a low-effort content cadence.
- **Instagram & TikTok** — same content repurposed; dog content has strong organic
  reach.
- **Community seeding** — local Chessington/Surrey dog-walking Facebook groups, pet
  shops, and dog groomers for word of mouth and possible stockist conversations.
- **Micro-influencer collabs** — UK dog-focused Instagram/TikTok accounts with
  5–20k followers, often willing to post for a free product rather than cash.
- **Seasonal drops** — Christmas dog-owner jumpers, summer "muddy paws" totes, to
  create recurring launch moments.

## 8. 90-Day Launch Plan

- **Weeks 1–2:** Finalise brand name/logo, design first 6–8 products, write brand
  voice guide.
- **Weeks 3–4:** Set up Etsy shop and/or simple landing page, connect POD supplier,
  order sample products to check quality before going live.
- **Week 5:** Soft launch to friends, family, and local dog-owner groups.
- **Weeks 6–8:** Start regular short-form content cadence (3–5 posts/week), track
  which designs get engagement.
- **Weeks 9–12:** Double down on best-performing designs, run one small paid ad test
  (~£50–100), evaluate whether to invest further or treat as a stable side income.

## 9. Legal & Compliance Checklist (UK)

- [ ] Register as a sole trader with HMRC (free) once trading starts.
- [ ] Keep income/expense records for Self Assessment.
- [ ] Publish Terms & Conditions, Privacy Policy, and a Returns Policy on the store
      (UK consumer law gives online buyers a 14-day cooling-off/right to cancel on
      most goods).
- [ ] Only use your own or properly licensed artwork — do not copy existing designs.
- [ ] If building an email list, use double opt-in and a clear privacy policy —
      no purchased or scraped contact lists (UK GDPR/PECR).
- [ ] Consider public liability insurance only if attending markets/events in person.

## 10. Risks

- **Platform dependency** — Etsy/Instagram policy or algorithm changes can hit
  traffic; mitigate by owning an email list and eventually a standalone store.
- **Design copyright** — must stay strictly original or properly licensed.
- **Seasonality** — gift-driven spikes around Christmas/Mother's Day; plan cashflow
  and content accordingly.
- **Time constraints** — as a side hustle, content cadence is the main bottleneck;
  automation (see `apps/youtube` in this repo) is the lever to pull if time is tight.
