# Resources & Contacts — Muddy Paws Co.

These are public business services and organisations relevant to setting up and
running the business. They are **not** a list of individuals' personal contact
details — no personal data has been collected or scraped for this document, in
line with UK GDPR/PECR. Each one requires you to personally sign up (identity
verification, payment details, or a business decision only you can make).

## Print-on-Demand Suppliers

| Supplier | Why | Link |
|---|---|---|
| **Printful** | Own UK fulfilment centre (Wolverhampton) — fastest, most consistent domestic shipping; strong branding tools | printful.com |
| **Printify** | Lowest base costs, connects to multiple UK print partners (e.g. Print Clever, Prodigi) — more choice, some quality variance by partner | printify.com |
| **Prodigi** | UK-headquartered, premium/fine-art print quality — best for higher-end products | prodigi.com |

**Recommendation:** start with Printful for consistency while validating designs;
revisit Printify once you know your bestsellers, to compare margins.

## Selling Platforms

- **Etsy (UK)** — lowest barrier to entry, no monthly fee, built-in buyer traffic for
  gift/craft niches. Good for validating demand first.
- **Shopify** — ~£25/mo, full control of the storefront and branding; better once
  you have proven products and want to reduce Etsy fee drag.

## Business Registration & Tax

- **HMRC — register as Sole Trader**: gov.uk "Set up as a sole trader" (free).
- **Companies House** — only needed if/when you incorporate as a Ltd company later.
  Online incorporation fee is £100 from 1 February 2026; confirmation statement is
  £50/yr.
- **HMRC Self Assessment** — annual filing once trading; deadline 31 January
  following the tax year.

## Business Banking (commonly used by UK sole traders/side hustles)

- **Starling Bank** — free business account, well regarded for sole traders.
- **Tide** — free tier, popular with small/side businesses, built-in invoicing.
- **Monzo Business** — free tier available, simple app-based banking.

## Accounting

- **FreeAgent** or **QuickBooks Self-Employed** — for tracking income/expenses once
  order volume makes a spreadsheet impractical.
- A simple spreadsheet is entirely sufficient at launch volume.

## Local & Business Support (Surrey / Chessington, KT9)

- **Royal Borough of Kingston upon Thames** — Chessington KT9 falls under this
  council; check their small business/startup support pages for any local grants
  or advice sessions.
- **Surrey Chambers of Commerce** — networking and small business support across
  Surrey.
- **gov.uk "Find business support"** tool — matches you to funding/advice schemes
  by postcode and business type.

## Legal Templates

- **gov.uk** guidance on distance-selling / consumer rights (14-day cooling-off
  period for online sales) — use as the basis for your Returns Policy.
- **Rocket Lawyer** or **Seed Legals** — template Terms & Conditions / Privacy
  Policy generators if you want a drafted starting point rather than writing from
  scratch.

## Design & Marketing Tools

- **Canva** — logo and product mockups (already used to produce the brand assets
  for this plan).
- Existing `apps/youtube` automation in this repo — can be repointed at a dog/pet
  content niche for organic short-form video marketing at low ongoing effort.
